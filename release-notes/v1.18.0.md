## LabelForge v1.18.0 — Alignment, finished

Building on the barcode alignment contributed by [@PaloTrucoo](https://github.com/PaloTrucoo) in v1.17.0, alignment is now complete across text, barcodes and the editor.

### ✨ What's new
- 🔤 **Text alignment** — left / center / right. In ZPL it uses the printer's native `^FB` field block, so centering is exact on paper.
- 🎯 **Center on label** — set `align` to center/right and leave `box_width_mm` empty: the whole label becomes the alignment area and `x_mm` acts as the margin on both sides. Variable‑length data stays centered.
- 🖱️ **Editor** — one‑click ⇤ ↔ ⇥ buttons, dashed guide of the alignment area with the centering axis, selection boxes that follow the real aligned position.
- 🖨️ **CPCL** — barcode alignment supported too.

### 🐛 Fixes
- Resize handle now adjusts bar height for Code39, Code93 and EAN‑13 (not only Code128).

### 🧪 Quality
- 6 new smoke tests for alignment; existing templates produce byte‑identical output to v1.17.0.

🙌 Thanks to [@PaloTrucoo](https://github.com/PaloTrucoo) for the original alignment work.
