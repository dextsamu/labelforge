## LabelForge v1.17.0 — Barcode alignment

### ✨ What's new
- 🎯 **Barcode alignment** — new `align` (left / center / right) and `box_width_mm` properties for Code128, Code39, Code93 and EAN‑13. The barcode is centered or right‑aligned inside a reference width, consistently in the preview and in every printer language (ZPL/TSPL/EPL/EZPL).
- Backward compatible: default is left, existing templates are unchanged.

🙌 Feature proposed and developed by **[@PaloTrucoo](https://github.com/PaloTrucoo)** (Juan Manuel) in his fork — thank you! Credited as co‑author of the commit, in the CHANGELOG and in the README contributors.
